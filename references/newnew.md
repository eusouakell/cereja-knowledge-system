# NewNew

Source:
- https://www.newnew.group/

Type: external reference.

## What Cereja is studying

NewNew is a reference for contextualized learning, co-creation and experience/journey design.

Public research questions include:
- how the organization describes contextualized learning;
- how it structures co-creation;
- how objectives, delivery and learning cycles are framed;
- what can be learned about designing solutions around a specific organizational context.

## Boundary

Cereja does not claim to use NewNew's methodology and does not reproduce proprietary material.

Any deeper commercial comparison, offer implications or positioning analysis belongs in private research.
