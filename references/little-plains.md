# Little Plains — Agentic Brand Systems

Source:
- https://agentic.littleplains.com/
- https://littleplains.com/

Type: external reference.

## What informed Cereja

Little Plains publicly describes agent-readable brand systems that combine brand guidance, design tokens/components, code, context and rationale, and tests them with fresh agents.

That work informed Cereja's exploration of:
- agent-readable brand/design context;
- explicit rationale behind design decisions;
- fresh-agent QA;
- maintaining a living system instead of a static guideline.

## Boundary

Cereja does not claim to use Little Plains' methodology. Núcleo, Flame and the Editorial Engine are independently developed and have their own scope, evidence model, accessibility requirements and governance.

Detailed commercial comparison and monetization analysis are intentionally not part of this public reference.
