# Public reference policy

Status: v0.1.

Cereja distinguishes **public provenance** from **private competitive/strategic research**.

## Public reference

A public reference answers:

> What external work materially informed this decision, and what did we learn from it?

Keep it concise:
- source and author/organization;
- observed idea or pattern;
- relevance to Cereja;
- boundary: what we are not claiming or copying.

Do not publish by default:
- competitor weakness analysis;
- detailed differentiation strategy;
- monetization hypotheses;
- pricing logic;
- offer architecture derived from comparisons;
- opportunity maps;
- internal scoring/ranking;
- full strategic synthesis.

## Private benchmark / intelligence

A private benchmark may contain:
- structured comparison;
- capability gaps;
- market positioning;
- commercialization implications;
- experiments to run;
- what to copy / avoid / differentiate from;
- internal conclusions.

Those materials should live in a private research workspace, not this public repository.

## Naming

Use `references/` for public provenance notes.

The former public `benchmarks/` pattern is deprecated. Existing benchmark files should be replaced by concise public references. Deleting or moving a public file does not erase copies or Git history; the policy is mainly for future protection and clearer boundaries.
