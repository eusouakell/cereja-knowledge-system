# Domain model

**Brand** — purpose, positioning and relationships between newsletter, consulting, events and audiovisual.

**Worldview** — durable interpretive lenses and principles.

**Editorial** — sections, voice, channels, formats and cadence.

**Audience** — readers, buyers and speaking/event audiences.

**Thesis Graph** — questions, hypotheses, theses, challenges and revisions.

**Evidence** — sources, scope, provenance and claim support.

**References** — concise public provenance notes: external source, observed pattern, relevance and boundary. Detailed benchmark/competitive analysis stays private.

**Consulting** — working operating model and method hypotheses.

**Offers** — experimental/validated offers and evidence.

**Projects** — public-safe project summaries and case-permission state.

**Archive** — published artifacts as historical evidence.

**Governance** — source authority, privacy, supersession and promotion.
