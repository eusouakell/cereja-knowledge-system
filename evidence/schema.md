# Evidence schema

Record:
- source ID;
- title;
- author/organization;
- URL/file;
- publication date;
- access date;
- source type;
- geography/population/scope;
- claims supported;
- rights/reuse;
- validation status.

For science/health/behavior also record:
- study type;
- sample/population when available;
- association vs mechanism vs intervention vs opinion;
- material limitations.

Do not universalize a study headline.
