# Chama reference board

References are **inspiration and pattern research**, not templates to copy.

## User-selected references

### Jeleiz
https://www.jeleiz.com/

What to study:
- confidence of motion;
- animation as part of identity;
- transitions that make the site feel authored.

Do not copy:
- exact compositions;
- assets;
- identity;
- animation choreography one-for-one.

### 21st.dev
https://21st.dev/

Use as:
- implementation and component-motion reference;
- discovery surface for authored React patterns;
- inspiration for heroes, transitions, interactive details.

Rule: imported/copied component ideas must be restyled through Chama tokens and reviewed for accessibility.

### Figcomponents
https://www.figcomponents.com/

Use as:
- component anatomy reference;
- Figma interaction/pattern inventory;
- benchmarking variants.

Rule: a popular component is not automatically a Cereja component.

### Khroma
https://www.khroma.co/

Use as:
- color exploration and adjacency testing.

Rule: generated palettes cannot become canonical brand colors without role definition and contrast review.

### 60fps.design
https://60fps.design/

Use as:
- motion taxonomy;
- interaction reference;
- study of microinteraction, reveal, morph, feedback and transitions.

Rule: prefer the motion principle over copying the visual treatment.

### getdesign / awesome-design-md
https://getdesign.md/
https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md

Use as:
- reference for agent-readable `DESIGN.md`;
- design-system structure;
- analysis of role-based tokens, components, motion and responsive behavior.

Rule: Chama is authored from Cereja's own identity. External DESIGN.md files are comparative references only.

## Additional editorial references to investigate later
