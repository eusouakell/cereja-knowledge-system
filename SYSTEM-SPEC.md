# System specification

## Purpose
Maintain Cereja knowledge for humans and agents without depending on chat memory.

## Rules
1. Founder decisions outrank prior suggestions.
2. Published content records what was said; it does not freeze a belief forever.
3. Newer timestamp does not equal higher authority.
4. Benchmarks do not become strategy automatically.
6. Neurodivergence may be a theme/offer without defining the whole brand.
7. Science and health-related claims require scoped evidence.
8. Client/private content stays outside the public repo.
9. Proprietary employer and client knowledge is not copied into this public system.
10. AI synthesis becomes canonical only after human promotion.

## Source states
`canonical` `approved` `working` `reference` `archive` `superseded`

## Certainty
`decision` `evidence` `hypothesis` `proposal` `unverified`
