# Provisional voice observations

Status: observation, pending Kell's approval. Basis: one public issue, [CF-027](../evidence/CF-027.md); this sample cannot establish the complete Cereja voice.

- Conversational opening and direct address.
- Named recurring sections help readers navigate diverse subjects.
- Cultural references connect technology to everyday imagination.
- A closing question invites participation.

Proposed brief constraint: use concrete editorial choices and accessible language; separate reported fact, personal interpretation and open question. Do not imitate private biographical details or treat a single issue's metaphors as mandatory style.

Approval required before promoting these observations to canonical voice guidance. Broaden to more public issues and record counterexamples first.
