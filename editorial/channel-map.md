# Channel map

**Newsletter** — deep curation, synthesis and thesis development.

**LinkedIn** — professional interpretation, concise arguments, consulting/speaking bridge and idea testing.

**Talks / workshops** — mature theses, live testing and collection of counterexamples/questions.

**Audiovisual / events** — founder intention; potential source of original interviews, reach and proprietary material.

**Consulting** — application of selected frameworks to client context.

Client work never becomes public editorial evidence automatically.
