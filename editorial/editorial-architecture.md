## Knowledge domains

### Culture
Media, design, entertainment, internet culture, creativity, social change.

### Technology
AI, software, platforms, emerging tech, digital work and innovation.

### Business
Organizations, work, strategy, startups, learning and transformation.

### Human Systems — working name
Neurodivergence, behavior, cognition, psychology/neuroscience research, learning science, communication, collaboration and future of work.

This fourth domain creates a bridge to consulting work without redefining Cereja as a neurodiversity-only brand.

## Rule
A section is a delivery format.
A domain is a knowledge context.
One edition can combine several domains.
